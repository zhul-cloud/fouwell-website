About Fouwell

10+ Years in Industrial Automation — Your Agile Multi-Brand Supply Partner.

Who We Are

Fuzhou Fouwell Technology Co., Ltd. is a professional trading company with over 10 years of expertise in industrial automation. We supply genuine PLC, HMI, servo motors, servo drives, inverters, sensors and spare parts from world-renowned brands including Siemens, Schneider, Mitsubishi, OMRON, Yaskawa, Allen-Bradley, ABB and more.

We deliberately stay lean. No layers of approval, no account-handoff queues — the person who answers your inquiry is empowered to solve your problem. Our lean operation also keeps fixed costs low, which translates into more competitive pricing for you.

"We may not be the biggest, but we are the ones who understand you best."
