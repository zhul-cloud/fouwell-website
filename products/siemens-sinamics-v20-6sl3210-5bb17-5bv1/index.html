Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What supply does 6SL3210-5BB17-5BV1 require?

Single-phase 200–240 V AC input. The packaging lists 10 A input and 50/60 Hz. This is not a three-phase 380–480 V input model; compare the complete order code and site supply.

What motor output is specified?

0.75 kW / 1 hp, three-phase output, 4.2 A on the photographed label. Output voltage is 0 to input voltage. Confirm the motor nameplate voltage, current, connection and application requirements.

Does this variant include a filter?

Yes. Siemens identifies this exact BV1 model as having an integrated C1 filter. Check the installation and EMC instructions; the filter does not establish compliance for every installation.

Which interfaces and controls are provided?

Siemens lists USS / Modbus RTU, a built-in BOP, 4 digital inputs, 2 digital outputs, 2 analog inputs and 1 analog output. Confirm parameter settings and wiring for the application.

What do the stock photos and price cover?

Fouwell confirms new original stock at CNY 1200 per unit. The photos show packaging marked Made in China, FS12 and FW03.98.02. They do not show an opened inverter or a powered test. Quantity, supplied configuration, shipping and warranty are confirmed in the quotation.

Can I replace any 0.75 kW inverter with it?

No direct interchangeability is assumed. Send the existing full model, supply, motor nameplate, control requirements, quantity and destination through https://www.fouwell.com/. Check current, voltage, filter, dimensions, wiring and parameters before selecting a replacement.
