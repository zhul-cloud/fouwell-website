Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which power supply does H3DK-GE use?

240–440 VAC at 50/60 Hz. Check the GE suffix; G and GA variants have different power ratings.

How are star time and transfer delay set?

Star time t1 uses 1–12 or 10–120 seconds. Select transfer delay t2 from 0.05, 0.1, 0.25 or 0.5 seconds.

Which contact ratings apply to the GE model?

Ith is 2 A. AC-15 ratings are 1.5 A at 120 VAC, 1 A at 240 VAC and 0.3 A at 440 VAC. Check the contactor control load and circuit design.

What is the condition, price and origin?

New original units are in stock at USD 164.25 each, as confirmed by Fouwell. The photographed unit and box are marked Made in China; OMRON is a Japanese brand. Confirm available quantity in the quotation.

Will it fit my existing starter?
