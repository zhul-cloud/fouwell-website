Frequently Asked Questions

Which exact model and article number are offered?

SSM.1.B1.300.61.S4, with A.No. G109391 on the photographed ENGLER label. Include both identifiers when asking for a replacement.

What can be checked in the photographs?

The photos show the identification label, threaded mounting body, rod, float and right-angle plug connector. They do not show a measured switching point, electrical load test or operation in a liquid.

Does 300 establish the overall installation length?

Do not use the model code alone as a measured overall length. Confirm the drawing for G109391, the mounting reference, switching point and required insertion dimensions before ordering.

Can generic SSM contact ratings and wiring be used?

Confirm the electrical rating and terminal diagram for this exact .61 version. An SSM datasheet for a different design is not sufficient to establish its voltage, current, pinout or switching action.

Which liquids and conditions are suitable?

Provide the medium, density, temperature, pressure and required switching function. Confirm wetted materials, seal, float suitability and installation orientation against the exact manufacturer specification; suitability for every liquid is not assumed.
