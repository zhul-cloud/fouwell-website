Typical Applications

VEGA PS6X.2SWYDBXATKMKHAXXXXXXX is typically used in the following applications:

🛢️
Bulk solids & silo level
80GHz focusing handles dusty conditions and internal obstructions (agitators, ladders) common in solids storage better than lower-frequency radar.
💧
Liquid storage tanks
Non-contact measurement with no moving parts — suited to tanks where fouling, foam or vapor would affect contact-based level sensors.
🏭
Process vessels with condensation/turbulence
The narrow beam angle and advanced signal processing filter out false echoes from condensation, agitator blades and turbulent surfaces.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the long order code on this VEGAPULS 6X mean?

It's VEGA's ordering code encoding antenna type (PP horn), process connection, output signal, seal material and other build options for this specific 80GHz radar level sensor. Send us your full nameplate code and we can confirm the exact configuration or find the closest sourceable match.

What's the difference between this VEGAPULS 6X and the VEGAPULS 64 you also sell?

They're different radar generations: the 6X operates at 80GHz with a narrower beam angle (better for tight nozzles and internal obstructions), while the 64 is VEGA's 26GHz compact line. They aren't interchangeable — the right choice depends on your tank geometry and process conditions, which we're happy to help confirm.

How do I configure or commission this sensor?

Through Bluetooth using the VEGA Tools smartphone app, or via PACTware with the VEGA DTM — no need to open the housing or bring a special programming cable to site.

What output signal does this unit provide?

4–20mA with HART, in a standard 2-wire loop-powered configuration — compatible with most existing analog level-monitoring loops and PLC analog inputs.
