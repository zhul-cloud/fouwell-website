Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which type matches ABB product ID 1SBL931074R8010?

It identifies AX25-30-10-80. Use the complete type and product ID together when requesting a replacement. AX25 alone does not specify the coil voltage or auxiliary-contact arrangement.

Is AX25 rated 25 A or 32 A?

The photographed label shows 25 A for AC-3 motor duty at 380–400 V AC and 32 A for AC-1 duty. Motor power at 380–400 V AC is 11 kW. Choose by the actual utilization category, voltage and operating conditions; the two current ratings are not interchangeable.

What supply does the R80 coil require?

220–230 V AC at 50 Hz or 230–240 V AC at 60 Hz, as shown on the coil and carton. Match both voltage and frequency to the control supply. The Ui 690 V marking is an insulation rating, not a coil supply value.

What contacts and accessories are included in the type?

Three normally open main poles and one built-in normally open auxiliary contact at 13–14. Additional contact blocks, overload relays and interlocks must be selected and quoted separately for the intended assembly.

Where was the photographed batch made?

The carton and side label are marked Made in China. The ABB France address identifies a company location and does not change the batch origin. Regional catalogue origin information can differ from the photographed units.
