Pilz PNOZXV1P-777602

Pilz PNOZ XV1P 777602 is a 24 V DC safety relay with two instantaneous normally open contacts and one delayed normally open contact. Its release delay is adjustable from 1 to 30 seconds. The 22.5 mm unit uses plug-in screw terminals. New original stock is available at USD 280 per unit.

Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Which exact PNOZ XV1P version is offered?

Order number 777602, designated PNOZ XV1P 30/24VDC 2n/o 1n/o t. Match the order number and full designation when requesting a replacement.

What is the supply voltage?

24 V DC, with 3.5 W DC power consumption. These values appear on the photographed label and in the Pilz product data.

Which contacts are instantaneous and delayed?

The relay has two instantaneous normally open safety contacts and one delayed normally open safety contact. The photographed front identifies 13–14 and 23–24 as instantaneous outputs and 37–38 as the delayed output. Follow the manufacturer manual for the application wiring.

What delay range applies to 777602?

Pilz lists a release time of 1–30 seconds for 777602. The photographed unit has a seconds dial up to 30. Confirm the required delay and exact order number rather than selecting by the PNOZ XV1P family name alone.

Does the relay alone establish machine safety compliance?

The unit supports monitoring of E-STOP, safety gates and light curtains. The complete safety function, input wiring, start/reset arrangement, contact loading and selected delay must be designed and validated for the machine using the manufacturer documentation.

What are the condition, origin and price?

Fouwell confirms new original stock at USD 280 per unit. This photographed batch is marked Made in France. Available quantity, delivery, shipping and warranty terms are confirmed in the quotation. Send order number 777602, required delay, quantity and destination through https://www.fouwell.com/.
