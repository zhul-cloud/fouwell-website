Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HORIBA 6108-50B?

A dome-type combination pH electrode, manufacturer part number 3200265000.

What is its measuring range?

HORIBA specifies pH 0–14 using the glass electrode method.

What process limits are listed?

−10 to 100 °C without freezing and 0–0.6 MPa. Check suitability for the actual process.

Which holders are listed?

The 48/96 series brochure lists CH-101, CF-251, CF-301 and CF-401. Confirm the exact installation.

Is it interchangeable with another 6108 model?

Do not assume interchangeability by series name. Compare the full model, cable, connection and installed arrangement.
