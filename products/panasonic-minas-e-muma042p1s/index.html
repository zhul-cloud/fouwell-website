Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main ratings of MUMA042P1S?

400 W, 1.3 N·m rated torque and 3000 r/min rated speed; maximum speed is 5000 r/min. The encoder is incremental, 2500 P/r.

Why does the label say 114 V while the catalogue says 200 V?

The catalogue specifies a 200 V voltage class, while the photographed motor nameplate reads three-phase AC 114 V, 2.5 A. These are different references. Use the specified servo driver and official wiring instructions; do not connect the motor directly to mains.

Does this model have a brake and oil seal?

The official MUMA042P1S specification lists neither a holding brake nor an oil seal. Check the full suffix, shaft and machine requirements when selecting a replacement.

Which drivers are listed by Panasonic?

Panasonic lists MLDET2310P and MLDET2510P, both discontinued. Confirm driver code, power supply, encoder interface, wiring and parameters. A matching power rating alone does not establish compatibility.

Is it discontinued, and what stock is offered?

Panasonic marks this model discontinued. Fouwell confirms new original units in stock at CNY 950 per unit. The photographed motor is marked Made in China. Available quantity, shipping and warranty terms are confirmed in the quotation.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the complete motor model, current driver model, nameplate photos, required quantity and destination through https://www.fouwell.com/. No direct replacement model is confirmed.
