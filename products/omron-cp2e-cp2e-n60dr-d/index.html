Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is OMRON CP2E-N60DR-D?

It is a CP2E network-model compact PLC CPU unit with 60 built-in I/O points: 36 digital inputs and 24 relay outputs. Retain the complete CP2E-N60DR-D model when comparing a replacement or preparing a quotation.

Does this version use DC supply and relay outputs?

Yes. The photographed side label states SOURCE 24 V DC, 20 W, and the manufacturer lists relay outputs for this model. Match both the CPU supply and output type against the installed controller. Relay and transistor variants are different configurations.

What Ethernet and option positions are provided?

The manufacturer lists two built-in Ethernet ports and two option-module positions. The photographed front identifies PORT1A and PORT1B, and the port close-up shows two RJ45 sockets. Option covers in a photo do not establish that optional serial or analog modules are included.

What is the program capacity and what should be checked for migration?

The manufacturer product listing specifies 10 ksteps of program memory. Before replacement, confirm the existing project, required functions, communication settings, expansion units and software requirements against the applicable CP2E documentation. This listing does not establish a drop-in replacement for another CP-series CPU.

What do the batch photographs show?

The carton and device side label identify CP2E-N60DR-D, version 1.0, lot 31726M and Made in China. Six photos show the carton and papers, front, terminals, side label and Ethernet ports. They do not show a running project, communication test or loaded relay-output test. Carton quantity 1 is a packing marking rather than available stock quantity.
