Buyer Questions, Engineer Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Kinco MD204L?

It is a programmable text display / HMI listed in Kinco's MD200/300 series.

What display does the MD204L use?

Kinco lists a 4.3-inch STN monochrome display with 192 × 64 pixels.

Is the MD204L a touchscreen?

No. Kinco describes a non-touch panel with 20 configurable membrane keys.

Which serial modes are listed?

Kinco's catalogue lists PC RS232 and PLC RS232/RS485/RS422 on COM0. Confirm the project protocol and cable pinout.

Which supply voltage should I use?

The available Kinco documents differ by hardware version. Check the unit label and matching manual before applying power.

How do I check availability and price?

Fouwell states new original stock at USD 53 per unit. Confirm available quantity, quotation validity and delivery terms with the team.
