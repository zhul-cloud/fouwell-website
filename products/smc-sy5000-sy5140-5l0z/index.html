Frequently Asked Questions

What is the exact model listed here?

SY5140-5L0Z is the user-specified internal model. SMC’s public catalog lists related model SY5140-5LZ; it does not confirm the additional “0” variant.

What series and valve configuration does SMC list for SY5140-5LZ?

SMC lists the SY5000 Series, 5-port, 2-position, single-solenoid, internal-pilot configuration for the related catalog number.

What is the rated voltage of the related catalog model?

The SMC catalog lists 24 VDC for SY5140-5LZ. Check the full marking on the supplied unit before connecting the coil.

What connector is listed?

The related SY5140-5LZ catalog entry specifies an L-type plug connector with a 300 mm lead. Confirm the actual wiring and connector before installation.

Can this valve replace another SY5000 model directly?

Direct interchangeability is not confirmed. Compare the full model, valve base/manifold, port thread, coil wiring and circuit requirements.

The user confirms new original condition, in-stock status and a price of US$40. Quantity and order terms should be reconfirmed when requesting a quote.
